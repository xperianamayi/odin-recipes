Garlic Chicken

How to make Garlic Chicken

Helo there! Thinking about making a quick appetizing meal for a suprise gift to your loved one? Think no further, below is a recipe that will do for you and your loved one. If they ask how? Be ready to show them this page. Thanks!

Ingredients

  • 2 teaspoons garlic powder, or to taste
  • 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • ½ cup seasoned bread crumbs
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 4 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves - pounded thin
  • 1 cup oil for frying, or as needed

Steps

  1. In a shallow dish, mix together the garlic powder, pepper, salt, paprika, bread crumbs and flour
  2. In a separate dish, whisk together the milk and egg
  3. Heat the oil in an electric skillet set to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C)
  4. Dip the chicken into the egg and milk, then dredge in the dry ingredients until evenly coated
  5. Fry chicken in the hot oil for about 5 minutes per side, or until the chicken is cooked through and juices run clear
  6. Remove from the oil with a slotted spatula, and serve.
